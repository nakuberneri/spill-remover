How it works and tips
  • The tool compares both tracks across the whole file and learns how the reference reaches the other mic: level, EQ, delay and polarity. It then builds that spill from the reference and subtracts it.
  • A DI or close mic of the spilling instrument works best. Small timing offsets (up to ~100 ms) are handled automatically.
  • It works best where the spill is a "linear" copy of the reference. Heavy amp distortion or a moving player leaves some residue; that's what Medium and Strong are for.
  • The cleaned file is 24-bit, the same length as your input. Drop it at the session start and it lines up.
